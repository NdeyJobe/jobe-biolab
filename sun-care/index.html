OTC Drug Facts

Sunscreen products sold in the United States are regulated as over-the-counter drugs. Required OTC Drug Facts and product information are coordinated according to the selected product and final manufacturing documentation.

Clients remain responsible for their broader brand marketing, advertising, distribution, and claims outside the approved product documentation.
