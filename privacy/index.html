Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. This policy applies to the Jobe Biolab website at jobebiolab.com.

Information we collect

When you submit an inquiry through our contact form, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, business email address, phone number, brand name, website, business stage, product preferences, quantity, and any additional details you share in the message field.

We do not collect payment information through this website.

How we use your information

Information submitted through the inquiry form is used to review your inquiry and respond with program details, pricing information, or a request for further information. We may also use your contact information to follow up on a previous inquiry.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Form processing

Inquiry forms on this website are processed by Formspree (formspree.io). By submitting the form, your data is transmitted to Formspree's servers before being delivered to us. Formspree's privacy policy is available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (pages.github.com). GitHub's privacy policy applies to traffic and data passing through GitHub infrastructure.

Email communications

If you contact us by email directly, we retain that correspondence to manage the business relationship. You may request that we delete your correspondence at any time by contacting us at info@jobebiolab.com.

Analytics

This website does not currently use third-party analytics tracking. If this changes, this policy will be updated.

Data retention

We retain inquiry information for as long as necessary to manage the business relationship or as required by applicable law. You may request deletion of your information by contacting us directly.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through this website. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information in transit.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@jobebiolab.com.

Contact
