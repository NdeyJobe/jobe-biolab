Process

A clear path from
inquiry to launch.

We've structured the private-label process to be straightforward and transparent — so you know what to expect at every stage before committing.

01

Inquiry

Complete the inquiry form to tell us about your brand. We review every inquiry individually before responding with the appropriate next step.

We collect

  • Brand name & contact
  • Website or social profile
  • Requested quantity
  • Target launch date
  • Desired product format
  • Packaging preferences
  • Target customer
  • Current business stage

02

Program review

Once we've reviewed your inquiry, we provide current program details including product specifications, customization options, pricing, packaging configuration, labeling requirements, and timeline expectations.

This is where you have the information needed to decide whether the program is the right fit for your brand — with no commitment required at this stage.

03

Brand assets

If you'd like to proceed, you submit your approved brand assets — logo, product name, label artwork, and any required product claims for review.

Client submits

  • Approved logo and brand name
  • Product name
  • Label artwork in required format
  • Any requested product claims for review

04

Review and approval

Before production begins, you review and approve all program details in writing. This includes product specifications, packaging configuration, artwork, label content, pricing, and timeline.

No production begins until written approval is received.

05

Payment and production

Production is confirmed after all required approvals and payment are received. Current payment terms are provided during the program review stage.

Timelines are estimates and are confirmed at the time of production based on current manufacturing availability.

06

Quality review and shipment

Before shipment, a pre-shipment review is conducted. You are notified when your order is ready to ship, along with tracking information and any relevant documentation.

Shipping and delivery details are confirmed during the program review stage based on your delivery address and order requirements.

Every stage is transparent before you commit.

Submitting an inquiry does not reserve inventory, confirm pricing, create a contract, or guarantee production availability. We provide full program details so you can make an informed decision.
